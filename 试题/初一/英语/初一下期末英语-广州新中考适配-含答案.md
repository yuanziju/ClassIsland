The Dragon Boat Festival is an important traditional Chinese festival. It is on the 5th day of the 5th lunar month. People celebrate it to remember the great poet Qu Yuan.

On this day, people eat zongzi. Zongzi is a kind of rice dumpling. It is made of sticky rice with meat or beans inside, wrapped in bamboo leaves. In different places of China, people have different kinds of zongzi. Some are sweet and some are salty.

Another important activity is dragon boat racing. A dragon boat is a long boat with a dragon's head at the front. Many people row the boat together. The races are very exciting. People cheer loudly for the teams.

In some places, people also hang mugwort leaves on their doors. They believe this can bring good luck and keep bad things away.

The Dragon Boat Festival is not only a festival, but also a part of Chinese culture. It shows the wisdom and traditions of Chinese people.
